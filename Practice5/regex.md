# Regular Expression Exercises

## Task 1 — Search
Use `re.search()` to find the word `Python` in a sentence.

## Task 2 — Find all words
Use `re.findall()` to extract all words from:
`Python, Java, C++, JavaScript`

## Task 3 — Digits
Find all integer numbers in:
`There are 25 students, 3 teachers and 2 assistants.`

## Task 4 — Email
Extract all email addresses from a text.

## Task 5 — Phone number
Find phone numbers in the format:
`123-456-7890`

## Task 6 — Date
Find dates in the format:
`DD.MM.YYYY`

## Task 7 — Replace
Replace every sequence of digits with `NUMBER`.

## Task 8 — Split
Split:
`apple,banana;orange|pear`
using comma, semicolon or vertical bar.

## Task 9 — Quantifiers
Match:
- `cat`
- `cats`
- `cattt`

Use `+` and explain what it does.

## Task 10 — Character classes
Find words that contain only letters and digits.

## Task 11 — Special sequences
Demonstrate:
- `\d`
- `\D`
- `\w`
- `\W`
- `\s`
- `\S`

## Task 12 — Start and end
Use `^` and `$` to find lines beginning with a selected word and ending with a selected character.

## Task 13 — Groups
Extract name and age from:
`Name: Anzor, Age: 18`

## Task 14 — Flags
Use `re.IGNORECASE` and `re.MULTILINE`.

## Task 15 — Receipt
Parse product names and prices from `raw.txt`.

The solutions are demonstrated in the files inside the `RegEx` folder and in `receipt_parser.py`.
